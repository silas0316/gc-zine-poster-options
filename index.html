The skill

gc-minimal-zine-poster v0.3 — a skill by LiamGvchi that turns a theme, sentence, article, mood, or photograph into a sparse paper-texture editorial poster. Released under the MIT License, © 2026 LiamGvchi.

Where these options come from

Every option on this page is transcribed from the skill's own reference files — references/variation-engine.md for the layout, anchor, typography, texture, and decoration axes, and references/style-system.md for the paper, color, and mood rules. Nothing here was invented.
